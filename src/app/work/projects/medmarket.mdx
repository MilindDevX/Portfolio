## Project Overview

MedMarket India solves three structural problems in the Indian OTC medicine retail market: counterfeit medicines, price opacity, and expiry losses. This platform provides a location-aware, multi-sided marketplace connecting verified pharmacies with consumers, built specifically for regulatory compliance under the Drugs & Cosmetics Act and CDSCO guidelines.

## The Problem

The local pharmacy market suffers from unverified inventory and MRP (Maximum Retail Price) violations. Pharmacies also deal with significant "dead stock" and expired medicines. We needed a robust platform with discrete portals for Consumers, Pharmacy Owners, and Admins to strictly enforce compliance while providing a seamless shopping experience.

## Technical Decisions & Architecture

To handle strict regulatory and performance requirements, I built the platform using **Node.js 20 / Express 5** for the backend and **React 19** for the frontend, heavily utilizing **Zustand** for global state.

- **Strict Role-Based Access Control**: Requests flow through a middleware pipeline (`authenticate` → `requireRole` → `Zod validate`).
- **DPCO Price Enforcement**: A strict PostgreSQL `CHECK` constraint (`selling_price ≤ MRP`) prevents price gouging at the database level.
- **Inventory & Expiry Logic**: Built automated backend services to trigger tiered alerts for expiring stock (60 days, 30 days) and detect dead stock based on sales inactivity.

## Measurable Outcomes

- **Verified Compliance**: Enforced CDSCO registration validation, eliminating counterfeit listings.
- **Robust Architecture**: Achieved comprehensive test coverage and clean service-repository separation, enabling rapid scaling.
- **Seamless Consumer Experience**: Integrated location-aware GPS discovery and a 5-stage real-time order tracking pipeline.
