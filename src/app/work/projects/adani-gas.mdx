## Project Overview

Built a comprehensive financial model and DCF valuation for Adani Total Gas Ltd. to structurally analyze its multi-year revenue growth and determine the true intrinsic value of its stock.

## Financial Modeling & Analysis

- **Consolidated Financial Model**: Constructed a 4-year (FY2022–FY2025) model structuring Income Statements, Balance Sheets, and Common Size statements directly from raw annual report data. This enabled deep trend and cross-sectional analysis across Rs.3,252Cr to Rs.5,457Cr revenue growth.
- **Ratio Analysis**: Computed and interpreted over 12 financial ratios across Liquidity, Efficiency, and Profitability. Surfaced insights on declining margin trends despite improving asset utilization.
- **Valuation Framework**: Built a DCF valuation model using both FCFF (Free Cash Flow to Firm) and FCFE (Free Cash Flow to Equity) frameworks.
  - Estimated Cost of Equity via CAPM (13.8%).
  - Calculated Terminal Value using India’s nominal GDP growth rate (8%).
- **Sensitivity Analysis**: Performed sensitivity analysis on a 5x5 grid varying Cost of Equity and Terminal Growth Rate, quantifying valuation ranges to communicate model uncertainty to non-technical stakeholders.

## Key Outcomes

- Successfully modeled the Expected Growth Rate using ROIC × Reinvestment Rate, distinguishing sector-wise applicability.
- Concluded the stock was critically OVERVALUED at Rs.602.6 vs. intrinsic value of Rs.154.7.
